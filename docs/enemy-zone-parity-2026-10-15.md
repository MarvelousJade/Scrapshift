## Corrections

### Use the body's circle and both coordinate offsets

The former implementation tested enemy origins against radius 2.00639. The reference tests circle/body overlap, so an enemy can enter while its origin remains outside the zone. The corrected `CombatGeometry.HealerZoneOverlaps` uses the zone radius plus the ally's physical **body** radius and compares their actual centers.

The project adapts source scenes to body-centered presentation. The collision importer already derived hurt/contact offsets relative to that adapted center. It now additionally exports each physical body's offset and its source-root offset using that same convention. For example:

| Enemy | Adapted body offset | Body radius | Source-root offset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Healer | `(0, -0.21)` | 0.162788 | `(0, -0.24)` |
| Baby alien | `(0, -0.21)` | 0.22 | `(0, -0.24)` |
| Butcher | `(0, -0.88)` | 0.24 | `(0, -0.88)` |
| Predator | `(0, -0.72)` | 0.4 | `(0, -0.88)` |

Thus the healer zone center is `healer.pos + OriginOffset("healer")`; an ally body center is `ally.pos + BodyOffset(ally.id)`. Incoming-hit and outgoing-contact circles are not substituted for the physical body. Logical collision geometry remains independent of visual squash/rotation.

### Apply the supplied collision mask to neutral trees

The former loop included every live object in the enemy list, including neutral trees. Imported `bodyLayer` metadata and the layer-4 check now exclude trees. Ordinary enemies, elites and bosses retain their source body-layer eligibility. No player or support-actor healing path was added.

### Remember full-health occupancy without fake healing feedback

Every overlapping eligible body is recorded in the healer's occupant set, even if already at maximum health. Healing and `supportFlash` are applied only when a body newly enters while damaged. Taking damage while remaining inside does not count as another entry. The body must fully exit and reenter before another heal. Each healer retains its own occupant set.
