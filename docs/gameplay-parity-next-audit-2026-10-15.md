## Rechecked interpretations

- Doc Moth and Potion contribute additively to the same regeneration-bonus array in the supplied resources. An initial multiplication hypothesis was rejected and its temporary edit reverted. No multiplication fix is retained.
- Ice Cube applies through the hurt callback before the triggering hit is resolved, so that hit receives the vulnerability. Its registered one-proc limit remains; this pass does not introduce repeated refreshes.
- Critical current-health and burning current-health bonuses belong after Unit's damage calculation in `enemy.gd:get_damage_value`; moving the boss multiplier does not move those bonuses. The earlier rejected Greek Fire interpretation applied its bonus to every ordinary hit against an already-burning enemy. The newly confirmed application-hit event is distinct and now handled.
- The supplied base resources inspected by the pickup agent did not register `scale_materials_with_distance`. The existence of a source hook alone does not establish a missing base-item mechanic.
