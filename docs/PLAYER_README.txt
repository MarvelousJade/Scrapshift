SCRAPSHIFT

Run Scrapshift.exe with its entire Windows folder beside it.
WASD / arrow keys: move. Weapons attack automatically.
Escape: back in menus, pause/resume in game. M: sound. Enter: confirm or next wave.
Collect green materials for currency and XP. At wave end, remaining materials fly to the money icon with rising amounts, then remain in stored reserves for later doubled pickups. Pending upgrades/crates appear at the top right; harvesting is shown beneath materials.
Crates heal 3 HP and reveal an item at wave end: Enter takes it, X recycles.
Crates resolve before levels. Remaining crates are collected automatically.
At wave end, choose queued stat upgrades with 1-4 or the mouse.
Shop: buy items/weapons and lock offers. Left/right-click an owned weapon for Combine, Recycle and Cancel.
R: paid reroll. Most characters have six weapon slots; some profiles change this.
161 initially obtainable item types, 46 available weapon families, up to six floating weapons.
Fifteen weapon sets count duplicates, regardless of tier; some weapons belong to two sets.
Start -> Random or Rookie / Pugilist / Hothead / Sharpshot / Arc Weaver -> starting weapon -> Danger 0.
The five initial profiles have distinct modifiers and starting choices. Forty-five further profiles have real challenge unlocks saved between sessions. Ordinary profiles retain the requested default weapon plus your chosen weapon; special zero/one-slot restrictions take precedence.
Weapon selection shows only unlocked choices eligible for that character, plus Random.
Pause/shop/level-up/crates: hover stats, owned items or weapons to inspect descriptions.
Tab / Shift+Tab cycle inspection targets; arrow keys move the selector. Move the mouse to return to hover.
Fullscreen is the default. F11 or Options > Video toggles fullscreen/windowed; your choice is saved.
New runs enter with a 1.2-second parachute landing before the wave timer starts.
Options: turn Reduced Motion off to see the full descent and squash animation.
The player has a smooth shell, swappable facial details and widely spaced, rounded silver boat-shaped tiny legs.
Reference-sized arena with soft curved earth edges, small ground details, a following camera and flat oval shadows.
Floating weapons rest toward your facing direction; rear slots sit behind the body.
Previously illustrated items alter visible features; newly added items currently use stat icons; enemies spring without arms or legs.
Watch enemy charge windups and ranged projectiles.
Survive twenty waves. The pause menu shows weapons, item effects, stats and the wave timeline. Audio and reduced motion are in Options.
The ending shows the attacker/result, then stats and gear. Restart keeps your starting selection; New Run returns to selection.

Original AI-generated sprites and a procedural salvage yard;
user-supplied Brotato reference sound effects, plus original terminal cues.
An engineering/portfolio study; not an official Brotato release.
Full-run balance and player acceptance remain unverified.

Combat update: distinct lower-body hitboxes, stronger pistol knockback, brief enemy slowing after contact, and clearer white impact flashes. Original artwork retained.

Cybernetic presentation: cream human-like mascot, implant names with reference values, footsteps/pickup feedback and tabbed options. All 62 base weapon families are registered; only implemented, source-unlocked tiers can appear. No DLC resources imported.

October 12 completed base catalog: all 209 items, 62 weapon families (205 tiers), 50 character profiles and 115 usable unlock challenges. Fresh saves start with five characters, 161 items and 46 weapon families / 160 tiers. Includes the nine companion implants and wave-changing effects. No DLC.

Harvesting grants materials and XP at wave end. Positive Harvesting normally increases by 5%, rounded up, for the following wave. Character/item modifiers can change this. The separate stored-material bag is hidden; leftover materials still bank for later pickups.

Shop items appear bottom left; weapons use three columns and two rows for six slots. Sort items by obtaining order, name, tier or category and reverse the order. White/blue/purple/red indicate tiers I/II/III/IV. Green values are beneficial changes, red values penalties; gold labels show weapon types. Hover or select equipment to inspect its live stats and weapon sets.

Validation and limitations: see docs/verification.md and artifacts/base-completion-review/review.html in the project. Automated tests and staged rendered checks are not human balance/playtesting. Companion and elite presentation uses our existing art and Unity-specific movement adaptations.
