## Changes and intent

- Replaced six sheets (130 slices including effects): items, stats, equipment, starter weapons, base weapons and wearables. Broad fills and simpler shading remove rusty patches without changing gameplay values. This does not provide a distinct illustration for every catalog item; some still share stat icons.

- Separated the main-menu plate and hero. The hero uses the gameplay spring curve at reduced amplitude; surrounding enemies slowly sway in alternating directions. The logo and controls remain stationary and the layers share aspect-preserving placement.
- Replaced the high repeating music lead with two lower, softer notes per bar. The 146 BPM beat and bass remain. Music still starts only on the first weapon-selection Start action.

## Bugs investigated

New artwork exceeded some previous sprite rectangles. A read-only alpha-component audit now derives padded bounds and strip masks; it updates import metadata without changing image pixels or asset identities. This also separates neighboring art where a simple horizontal gutter search failed.

Custom sprite geometry produced Editor-test errors. Separate rectangular child sprites replaced that approach for overlapping atlas parts. A stationary-base check also exposed an initial Z-coordinate change from -1 to 0; preserving the spawn depth fixed it.

These are useful interview examples: distinguish an import-boundary defect from an art defect, then verify both the shared renderer and actual built-player behavior rather than relying on a screenshot alone.
