# Victory sequence

## Original game reference

The supplied Brotato 3.x project sets `RunData.run_won` when the final timed wave ends with a living player (`main.gd::_set_run_states`). `clean_up_room()` plays `ui/sounds/end_wave.wav` immediately and starts a four-second end-wave timer. A living player starts the `won` animation after 0.25 seconds (`entities/units/player/player.gd`). At 1.25 seconds, the player creates `particles/starship_beam.tscn`, whose sound is `starship_beam_won.wav`. The animation calls `run_won_screen` at 1.4 seconds from cleanup; the game then plays `Fear 10.mp3` at -5 dB and types `RUN_WON` with two bubble sounds. At four seconds, `main.gd::_on_EndWaveTimer_timeout()` changes to the end-run scene. That scene displays stats, weapons, progress rewards, items, and Restart / New Run / Main Menu buttons. Its victory title includes the zone and difficulty without repeating wave 20.

## Scrapshift implementation

The final wave keeps the arena visible for four seconds. The player performs a squash and jump. At 1.25 seconds, 25 copies of the original blue circle sprite begin rising in sequence through the beam as the player ascends, accompanied by the original beam sound. Their sizes vary within the original particle system's 49% random scale range. The player clears the top of the screen before the victory and letter sounds play with the `RUN WON` label at 2.05 seconds. The results screen opens automatically after four seconds. The beam sound and circle sprite are copied byte-for-byte from the supplied game and recorded in `artifacts/audio-parity-provenance.json`. `--win-review <folder>` captures the jump, ascent, label, beam, and results for visual review.

The player motion and rising circles recreate the main beats of the reference. The ascent is deliberately faster and the label appears later than the original sequence so the player has left the screen before it appears, as requested. The animation is not a frame-for-frame port of Godot's seven-second animation or its three particle systems. The results screen retains Scrapshift's existing stats, inventory, and progression presentation, so its detailed contents are not identical to Brotato's end-run scene.
