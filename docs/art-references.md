# Art and animation references

References researched October 10, 2026. These inform design choices; no game sprites, branding, models or audio are imported.

| Reference | What to study | Scrapshift interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Brotato](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1942280/Brotato/) | Readable player/enemy silhouettes, quiet ground, distinct pickups, immediate combat feedback | Small robots on a low-contrast deck, gold scrap, short impact bursts, automatic targeting |
| [Into the Breach](https://store.steampowered.com/app/590380/Into_The_Breach/) | Compact armored robot forms and clearly differentiated unit silhouettes | A repair bot with treads, visor and antenna; triangular runners; broad heavy drones. Top-down geometric meshes rather than reproducing its isometric pixel art |
| [Nova Drift](https://store.steampowered.com/app/858210/Nova_Drift/) | Abstract weapon geometry and bright effects against a dark playfield | Mint bolts, amber orbiting blades, an expanding pulse ring; restrained effects preserve enemy readability |

## Motion design

The following are our chosen motions, not frame-by-frame reproductions of reference animations: movement squash at a small amplitude, lateral player tilt, a 0.6-second spawn scale telegraph, impact expansion, brief damage blinking, and short-lived directional sparks. A reduced-motion option disables movement squash, tilt and camera shake. It does not disable functional weapon rotation or all flashing.

## Asset provenance

Native Unity meshes and flat-color materials generated by `ContentBuilder.cs`; robot/effect prefabs composed from those meshes. Synthesized short audio tones in `ArenaGame.cs`. Unity's built-in runtime GUI font. No third-party asset downloads or external asset licence dependency in the game content. Unity and test packages retain their own terms; the embedded AssetScope package is the existing companion project.

## Art scope

This is a cohesive small geometric style, not hand-painted production art. It can later be replaced with commissioned or licensed sprites while retaining gameplay definitions and prefab references. Art polish still needs rendered inspection and actual player feedback.
